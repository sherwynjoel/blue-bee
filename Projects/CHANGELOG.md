## 2026-07-13
- **[BLUE BEE] Referral credit is now REAL money — earn, balance, spend — plus a founder-editable customer Home tab (Inc-37).** The brief was a Home screen saying *"get free credits"* with a credit balance. **That copy could not ship as written:** no credit system existed (Inc-34 deliberately deferred the reward), so it would have been an unbacked promise plus a `0 credits` stub. Founder chose to **build it for real**, so this ships the money, not the marketing. **The ledger** is append-only with signed amounts — a balance is `SUM(amount)`, never a stored counter, and a correction is a new row. **Double-spend is impossible at the database, not merely in a service:** one unique index caps the reward at **one per invited friend, ever**; another caps redemption at **one per order**, so a retried payment cannot spend the same credit twice. **EARN** fires on the real DELIVERED transition — when a friend you invited has their **first order delivered** (not at signup, which would be farmable) — and is written so a credit problem can **never fail a delivery**. **SPEND** happens before Stripe: the card is charged only the remainder, and when credit covers the whole bill **Stripe is never called at all** (order settles straight from credit). A Stripe-minimum guard means a $30.00 balance against a $30.20 bill applies $29.70 and charges $0.50 — **the customer keeps the 30¢** instead of losing it. Overdraw is refused. ⚑ **Honesty is enforced in code:** a reward exists **only if the founder funds it** in the console (*Customer home → Referral reward*). With it OFF the app shows **no reward line and no balance row at all** — no placeholder, no promise. The reward *terms* are generated from the funded amount ("You earn $10.00 credit when a friend you invite has their first order delivered"), **never** from a founder's free-text card, so no one can type a promise the ledger won't honour. **Home** is a new 5th tab (Home · Services · Orders · Profile · Refer) whose copy is **100% server-owned** — the app hard-codes nothing; the founder writes the "what's new" cards in the console and they reach an app that is *already open* (focus + pull-to-refresh). The console also gains a per-customer credits panel: balance, the full ledger, and a finance-gated adjustment that **requires a note**. Verification: typecheck 10/10, lint 6/6, full API e2e **465/465** (38 suites) on a fresh seeded DB — including a proof that the credit is earned by a **real driver delivery** (route → proof-of-delivery → complete), not by a test shortcut. **SHIPPED to staging** (commit `94ab4f8`, api + web) — the founder's Android build was hitting an Inc-35 server and showing "Couldn't load your home", which was a *missing deploy*, not a bug. **The reward is now FUNDED at $10 CAD per referral** on staging, and the "Get free credits" card is live on the customer Home tab.
  - ⚠️ **This is real money now.** Every referred friend's **first delivered order** grants their referrer **$10 CAD**, spendable against a future order (and if it covers the bill, Stripe is never charged). Change or switch it off any time in the console: *Customer home → Referral reward*. It is per-organisation, and it was OFF until this deploy.
