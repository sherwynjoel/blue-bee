## What it is
Blue Bee is a commercial laundry & specialty-cleaning operating system for the Greater Toronto Area (PRD codename "Cascade"). One backend, four client surfaces (Customer app, Owner app, Driver app, Web console), three verticals built in order: specialty cleaning → care-home linen → hotels. It is the build for the GTA laundry market analysis at `Projects/CMO/laundry-canada/`.
