### The wired scorecard tile (replaces the Phase-2 placeholder)
The `costCenterSplit` Metric is `unavailable_in_phase_1` server-side (honesty firewall — never faked), so the tile no longer reads that Metric. Instead a new `CostCenterSplitStat` fetches the **real cost-centre report for the scorecard's exact window** and shows the amount split across centres + the centre count, or an honest **"—" / "No split this period"** when nothing is attributed. Wired in **both** scorecards (`AccountDetail.ScorecardCard` + `AnalyticsHome.ScorecardSection`).
